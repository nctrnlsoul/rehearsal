What a pass here does not prove

  • Live PayPal. Everything runs against the sandbox. A pass is evidence about your code's handling, not a promise about production.
  • Timing you can count on. PayPal's sandbox event list once lagged its own delivery by more than 2 min. When evidence does not arrive in time, the verdict is INCONCLUSIVE and you rerun.
  • Every dispute stage. Disputes open and wait on the merchant. The sandbox refuses require-evidence and adjudicate at the stage a new dispute starts in, so those are out of scope.
  • Every failure class. Proven on real runs: duplicate-webhook and refund-after-partial. Unclaimed-payout and overdue-invoice exist but are not claimed here. Dispute-on-a-refunded-order cannot be reached in this version and is reported as not tested.
  • Load. This rehearses business events one at a time. It is not a load or volume test.
